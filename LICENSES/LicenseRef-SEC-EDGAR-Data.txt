The files data/sec/*/companyfacts-*.json hold data from the U.S. Securities and Exchange
Commission's EDGAR system: the XBRL company facts API at https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/, retrieved on
2026-11-06 and trimmed to the concepts Proofsheet reads. Every fact inside a kept concept is unchanged.

The figures are what each company filed with the SEC, which publishes them as public information. They are not the
work of the Proofsheet author, and the MIT license that covers the rest of this repository does not apply to them.
